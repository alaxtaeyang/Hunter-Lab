## Part 1: SDS Gel Making

### Gel Preparation (10% Gel)
*Note: Different gel percentages will require adjusted volumes.*

Add the following reagents to two separate 50 mL tubes:

| Reagent | Resolving Gel | Stacking Gel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Water (nanopure)** | 15.6 mL | 5.55 mL |
| **30% Acrylamide (37.5:1 Bis)** | 13.3 mL | 1.7 mL |
| **Tris Buffer** | 10 mL (pH 8.8) | 2.5 mL (pH 6.8) |
| **10% SDS** | 400 µL | 100 µL |
| **10% APS** | 400 µL | 100 µL |

### Casting the Gel
1. **Pour Resolving Gel:** Add **25 µL TEMED** to the resolving gel tube and gently invert several times to mix. Pour up to the green clamp holding the two glass pieces together.
   > **Tip:** Slightly overfilling past the green line helps compensate for minor leakage. Waiting a few seconds after mixing before pouring can also reduce leaks, but do not wait too long as the gel is actively polymerizing and will become too viscous.
2. **Remove Bubbles:** Add **300–400 µL Isopropanol** to the top of the resolving gel to eliminate air bubbles.
3. **Polymerize:** Wait **~25 minutes** for the gel to fully solidify (check the remaining gel in the tube as a guide). Once solid, pour off/remove the isopropanol.
4. **Pour Stacking Gel:** Add **5 µL TEMED** to the stacking gel tube and gently invert several times to mix. Pour immediately up to the top line of the gel glass.
   > **Tip:** The stacking gel solidifies much faster than the resolving gel—pour quickly.
5. **Insert Combs:** Immediately place the gel combs into the stacking gel. Be careful to avoid trapping air bubbles, as air pockets will disrupt protein separation during PAGE.
6. **Polymerize & Store:** Wait **~25 minutes** for the gel to solidify. Once solid, remove the glass plates from the clamps, wrap them in a water-soaked paper towel covered in plastic wrap, and store at **4°C (fridge)**.
