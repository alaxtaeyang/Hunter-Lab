# Autoclaving

## 1. Liquids Cycle
1. **Rinse:** Rinse beakers and flasks thoroughly with **DI water**.
2. **Fill:** Fill the containers approximately **1/4 full with Milli-Q water**.
3. **Cover:** Cover openings with aluminum foil. *(Note: If using containers with caps, place the cap loosely on top first, then cover with foil.)*
4. **Label:** Apply a piece of **autoclave tape** across the top of the foil. *(Stripes will turn black once sterilization is complete.)*
5. **Load:** Using heat-resistant autoclave mittens, place the items inside the chamber. Close the door and click **Seal Door**.
6. **Run Cycle:**
   * **Mode:** Liquids
   * **Exposure Time:** 20 minutes
   * Click **Start**.

---

## 2. Dry Goods Cycle (No Liquids)
1. **Cover:** Cover empty beakers and flasks with aluminum foil. *(Note: If using containers with caps, place the cap loosely on top first, then cover with foil.)*
2. **Label:** Apply a piece of **autoclave tape** across the top of the foil. *(Stripes will turn black once sterilization is complete.)*
3. **Load:** Using heat-resistant autoclave mittens, place the items inside the chamber. Close the door and click **Seal Door**.
4. **Run Cycle:**
   * **Mode:** Wrapped
   * **Exposure Time:** 20 minutes
   * **Exhaust / Dry Time:** 20 minutes
   * Click **Start**.
