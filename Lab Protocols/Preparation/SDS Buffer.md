### 10X SDS Running Buffer (1 L)
1. Add **800 mL water** to a 1 L beaker with a stir bar.
2. Add **144 g Glycine** and **33 g Tris-Base**. Cover with foil and stir until the solution becomes clear.
3. Add **10 g SDS**, re-cover with foil, and stir until completely clear again.
4. Transfer to a 1 L graduated cylinder and fill to **1 L** with water.

### 1X SDS Running Buffer (1 L)
* Dilute **100 mL of 10X SDS Buffer** with **900 mL water**.
