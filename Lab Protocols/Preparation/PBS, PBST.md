### 10X PBS (1 L)
1. Add **800 mL MilliQ water** to a 1 L beaker with a stir bar.
2. Add the following reagents and stir until dissolved:

| Reagent | Amount (g) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sodium Chloride ($\text{NaCl}$)** | 80.0 g |
| **Potassium Chloride ($\text{KCl}$)** | 2.0 g |
| **Sodium Phosphate Dibasic Heptahydrate ($\text{Na}_2\text{HPO}_4 \cdot 7\text{H}_2\text{O}$)** | 11.5 g |
| **Potassium Phosphate Monobasic ($\text{KH}_2\text{PO}_4$)** | 2.0 g |

3. Bring volume to slightly under **1000 mL** with MilliQ water.
4. **Check pH:** Take **1 mL** of this 10X stock into a 50 mL tube and dilute to **10 mL** with MilliQ water (1X). The pH should read **7.2–7.4**. If needed, adjust the 10X stock to **pH 7.4** using $\text{HCl}$.
5. Transfer to a 1 L graduated cylinder, rinse the beaker with MilliQ water, and bring the total volume to **1 L**.

### 1X PBS (1 L)
* Combine **100 mL of 10X PBS** with **900 mL MilliQ water** in a graduated cylinder. *(Alternatively, scale the 10X recipe down by 10x and verify pH).*

### 1X PBST (500 mL)
1. Pour **500 mL of 1X PBS** into a bottle.
2. Using a wide-bore 1000 µL pipette tip, add **500 µL Tween-20** (very viscous) and stir for several minutes until completely mixed.
