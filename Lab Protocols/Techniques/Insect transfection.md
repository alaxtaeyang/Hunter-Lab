# Sf9 Insect Cell Transfection Protocol

## Part 1: Insect Cell Culture Preparation

1. **Seed Cells:** In a 6-well culture plate, seed **$9 \times 10^5$ Sf9 cells per well** in **2 mL** of complete growth medium.
   * *Example: If your stock culture is at $1 \times 10^6\text{ cells/mL}$, add **900 µL** of cells to reach $9 \times 10^5$ cells (note: protocol text states 100 µL—adjust based on exact stock concentration to hit $9 \times 10^5$ total cells per well).*
2. **Attach Cells:** Incubate the plate at **27°C for 1 hour** to allow the cells to attach.
3. **Wash Cells:** While the DNA:Lipid complex is incubating (Part 2), aspirate the media from the cells and wash with **2 mL of unsupplemented Grace’s Medium**.
   * *Note: Only perform this wash if dead cells are visible.*

---

## Part 2: DNA:Lipid Complex Preparation

1. **Dilute Bacmid DNA:** Dilute your bacmid DNA sample in **100 µL of unsupplemented Grace’s Medium** to a final amount of **1 µg DNA** ($0.01\text{ µg/µL}$ or $10\text{ ng/µL}$).
2. **Dilute Cellfectin:** Mix the Cellfectin Reagent thoroughly by inverting the tube several times. Dilute **6 µL of Cellfectin** into **100 µL of unsupplemented Grace’s Medium**.
3. **Combine & Incubate:** Combine the diluted Cellfectin with the diluted bacmid DNA (~200 µL total volume). Mix gently by tapping the tube a few times, then incubate at **room temperature for 1 hour**.
4. **Dilute Complex:** After the 1-hour incubation, add **600 µL of unsupplemented Grace’s Medium** to each Bacmid:Cellfectin tube (~800 µL total volume). Mix gently by tapping the tubes a few times.

---

## Part 3: Transfection

1. **Add Complex to Cells:** Add the entire DNA:Lipid complex mixture (~800 µL) dropwise to each well containing the Sf9 cells.
2. **Transfect:** Incubate the plate at **27°C for 5 hours**.
3. **Replace Media:** Aspirate the DNA:Lipid transfection mixture and replace it with **2 mL of complete growth media** per well.
4. **Incubate for Expression/Virus Production:** Incubate the cells at **27°C for 72 hours**.
