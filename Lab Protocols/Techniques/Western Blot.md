## Part 1: SDS-PAGE

1. **Assemble Apparatus:** Load the glass plates with the gel onto the central SDS core (with the golden rods), carefully remove the comb, and place the assembly into the SDS tank.
2. **Add Buffer:** Pour **~500 mL of 1X SDS-PAGE running buffer** into the central inner chamber until it fills to the top, then pour the remainder into the outer tank up to the green fill line.
3. **Prepare Samples:** 
   * Place target samples into microfuge tubes.
   * Briefly warm the **4X loading dye** at 95°C for 1–2 seconds, then add it to your samples to achieve a **1X final concentration**.
   * Heat samples at **95°C for 5 minutes**, then remove.
4. **Load & Run:** Using a micropipette, load the protein ladder (typically **3–4 µL**) and your samples (typically **15 µL**, depending on concentration). Run the gel at **100 V**.

---

## Part 2: Western Blotting (Transfer)

1. **Prepare Transfer Buffer:** Mix **700 mL water**, **200 mL Methanol**, and **100 mL 10X Western blot buffer**.
2. **Soak Components:** Pour the transfer buffer into a tray and soak:
   * 2 sponges
   * 6 filter papers (3 per sponge)
   * 1 nitrocellulose membrane
   * The SDS gel (remove from the glass plates after PAGE finishes and soak briefly)
3. **Assemble Transfer Sandwich:** Layer the components onto the transfer cassette in the following order (from bottom to top):
   * **Black side down**
   * Sponge
   * 3 filter papers
   * SDS gel
   * Nitrocellulose membrane *(use a roller to gently push out any air bubbles)*
   * 3 filter papers *(use the roller again to remove air bubbles)*
   * Sponge
   * **White side up**
4. **Run Transfer:** Close the cassette clamp and place it into the red-and-black electrode module inside the Western blot tank. Add **1 L of transfer/running buffer** and a frozen **ice pack**. Run at **100 V for 1 hour**.

---

## Part 3: Washing and Staining

1. **Block Membrane:** Disassemble the transfer cassette and retrieve the nitrocellulose membrane (the prestained protein ladder should now be visible on the membrane). Block in **PBS-Milk** (**1.5 g milk powder in 50 mL 1X PBS**) for **20 minutes** on a slow shaker.
2. **Primary Antibody Incubation:** Place the blot into a plastic pouch and heat-seal three sides. Add your primary antibody diluted in PBS-milk, squeeze out excess air, and seal the fourth side. Incubate **overnight at 4°C (cold room)** on a slow shaker.
3. **Wash:** The next day, remove the blot and wash **3 times for 10 minutes** each in **1X PBST**.
4. **Secondary Antibody Incubation:** Incubate the blot with the secondary antibody diluted in **10 mL PBS-milk** for **1 hour** on a slow shaker at room temperature.
5. **Final Wash & Image:** Wash **3 times for 10 minutes** each in **1X PBST**. Your blot is now ready to image.
