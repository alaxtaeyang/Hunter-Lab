# DNA Miniprep

## 1. Cell Harvesting
1. **First Spin:** Add **1.5 mL** of bacterial culture to a microfuge tube.
2. **Centrifuge:** Spin at **4,000 RPM for 10 minutes**.
3. **Remove Media:** Pour off the orange/brown supernatant into a waste beaker and gently tap the inverted tube onto a tissue paper to remove residual liquid.
4. **Second Spin:** Add another **1.5 mL** of bacterial culture to the same tube *(3.0 mL total culture used)*.
5. **Pellet Cells:** Centrifuge again at **4,000 RPM for 10 minutes** and discard the supernatant. *(Plasmid DNA is contained inside the cell pellet).*

---

## 2. Lysis & Neutralization
1. **Resuspend:** Add **250 µL of Resuspension Solution** to the cell pellet. Pipette up and down until no visible pellet remains.
2. **Lyse:** Add **250 µL of Lysis Solution** to the tube. Mix gently by inverting the tube **4–6 times**. *(Do not vortex, as this can shear genomic DNA.)*
3. **Neutralize:** Add **350 µL of Neutralization Solution** to all tubes first, then invert them all together **4–6 times**.
4. **Clarify Lysate:** Centrifuge the tubes (~850 µL volume + cellular debris) at **14,000 RPM for 15 minutes**. *(Plasmid DNA is now in the clear supernatant).*

---

## 3. Column Binding, Washing & Elution
1. **Bind DNA:** Transfer the clear supernatant into a spin column. Centrifuge at **14,000 RPM for 1 minute**, then discard the flowthrough.
2. **Wash 1:** Add **500 µL of Wash Solution** to the spin column. Centrifuge at **14,000 RPM for 1 minute** and discard the flowthrough.
3. **Wash 2:** Add another **500 µL of Wash Solution** to the spin column. Centrifuge at **14,000 RPM for 1 minute** and discard the flowthrough.
4. **Dry Column:** Centrifuge the empty spin column at **14,000 RPM for 1 minute** to remove residual ethanol.
5. **Elute DNA:** Transfer the spin column to a clean microfuge tube and add **50 µL of Elution Buffer** directly to the center of the membrane.
6. **Collect:** Centrifuge at **14,000 RPM for 2 minutes**. Save the flowthrough *(this is your purified plasmid DNA)*.

---

## 4. NanoDrop DNA Quantification
1. **Initialize:** Open the NanoDrop software and initialize the instrument using **2 µL of water**.
2. **Blank:** Wipe the pedestal, load **2 µL of Elution Buffer** *(matching the buffer your DNA is eluted in)*, and click **Blank**. Confirm the reading registers as **0.0 ng/µL**.
3. **Measure Samples:** Wipe the pedestal, load **2 µL of your purified DNA sample**, and measure.
4. **Verify Purity:** Check that the absorbance spectrum peaks cleanly at **260 nm** *(and verify standard purity ratios: **A260/A280 ≈ 1.8** and **A260/A230 ≈ 2.0–2.2**)*.
