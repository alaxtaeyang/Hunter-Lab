# Protein Lysis, Ni-NTA Purification & SDS-PAGE Protocol

## 1. Cell Lysis & Clarification
1. **Prepare Lysis Buffer:** Make **25 mL** of Lysis buffer.
2. **Resuspend:** Resuspend the protein pellet in a small volume (~**2.5 mL**) of Lysis buffer.
3. **Osmotic Lysis:** Add ~**400 µL of Glycerol** and **400 µL of NaCl** to induce lysis.
4. **Incubate:** Incubate on ice for **20 minutes**.
5. **Centrifuge:** Spin at **15,060 rpm** at **4°C** for **30 minutes**.
6. **Collect:** Save the supernatant for binding, and set aside a small amount of the **pellet** for SDS-PAGE analysis.

---

## 2. Ni-NTA Bead Preparation
*(Perform during the 30-minute centrifugation step)*
1. **Water Wash:** Wash the ethanol-suspended Ni-NTA beads with water. Allow the beads to settle, then aspirate/remove the liquid.
2. **Equilibration:** Equilibrate the beads with **Binding Buffer**. Allow the beads to settle, then remove the buffer.

---

## 3. Protein Binding & Washing
1. **Bind:** Combine the clarified supernatant and equilibrated Ni-NTA beads in a **15 mL tube**.
2. **Incubate:** Incubate with rotation/spinning in the cold room (**4°C**) for **1 hour**.
3. **Collect Flowthrough:** Centrifuge at **2,000 rpm** at **4°C** for **5 minutes**. Save the supernatant in a separate tube labeled **Flowthrough**.
4. **Wash 1:** Add Binding Buffer to the beads, centrifuge (**2,000 rpm, 4°C, 5 minutes**), and save the supernatant in a separate tube.
5. **Wash 2:** Repeat the Binding Buffer wash and centrifugation (**2,000 rpm, 4°C, 5 minutes**). Discard the supernatant. *(Target protein is now bound to the beads).*

---

## 4. Elution Gradient Setup & Column Collection

### Elution Buffer (EB) Gradient Preparation

| Elution Step | Elution Buffer (EB) | Imidazole | Total Volume |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **EB 1** | 975 µL | 25 µL | 1,000 µL |
| **EB 2** | 950 µL | 50 µL | 1,000 µL |
| **EB 3** | 900 µL | 100 µL | 1,000 µL |
| **EB 4** | 800 µL | 200 µL | 1,000 µL |

### Fraction Collection
1. Transfer the washed Ni-NTA beads to a gravity/purification column.
2. Collect **1 fraction** each using **EB 1**, **EB 2**, and **EB 3** *(Fractions 1–3)*.
3. Collect **3 consecutive fractions** using **EB 4** *(Fractions 4, 5, and 6)*.

---

## 5. SDS-PAGE Sample Preparation & Gel Run
1. **Aliquot Samples:** Take **50 µL** each of:
   * Supernatant (lysate)
   * First Flowthrough
   * Elution Fractions 1–6
   * *Plus a small sample of the saved lysis pellet*
2. **Denature:** Add **15 µL of 4X SDS Loading Dye** to each sample and boil at **95°C for 5 minutes**.
3. **Load 10% SDS Gel:**
   * **Ladder:** 3.5 µL
   * **Controls:** Pellet, Supernatant, Flowthrough
   * **Elutions:** 20 µL each of Fractions 1–6
4. **Stain & Destain:** Once the gel finishes running, submerge it in **Coomassie staining solution for 30 minutes**, then replace with **destain solution**.
