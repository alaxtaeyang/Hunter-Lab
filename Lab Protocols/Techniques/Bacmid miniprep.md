# Bacmid Transformation & Miniprep Protocol

## Part 1: Transformation into DH10Bac Cells

1. **Thaw Cells:** Thaw DH10Bac competent cells on ice for **10 minutes**. Once thawed, aliquot **~50 µL** into 14 mL Falcon tubes.
2. **Add DNA:** Add **2 µL** of plasmid DNA (gene of interest) to the 50 µL of cells. Mix gently, then incubate on ice for **30 minutes**.
3. **Heat Shock:** Heat shock at **42°C for 90 seconds**, then immediately cool on ice for **2 minutes**. 
   * *Note: Pre-warm SOC media to 42°C during this step.*
4. **Recovery:** Add **950 µL** of pre-warmed SOC media to each Falcon tube. Incubate at **37°C** on a rotating wheel for **~3 hours**.
5. **Serial Dilution:** Perform two consecutive 1:10 dilutions in LB media:
   * **1:10 dilution:** 10 µL of culture + 90 µL LB
   * **1:100 dilution:** 10 µL of the 1:10 dilution + 90 µL LB
6. **Plating:** Plate **100 µL** of the diluted cells onto LB SF9 plates. Incubate at **37°C for 48 hours**.
7. **Inoculation:** Pick isolated colonies and inoculate into **5 mL LB media** supplemented with **1X Gentamicin, Kanamycin, and Tetracycline**. Incubate overnight at **37°C** with shaking.

## Part 2: Bacmid DNA Isolation

1. **Harvest Cells:** Transfer **1.5 mL** of overnight culture into a microcentrifuge tube. Centrifuge at **4,000 rpm for 10 minutes**. Discard the supernatant (the intact cells—and your DNA—are in the pellet).
   * *Optional: To increase yield, add another 1.5 mL of culture to the same tube and repeat the spin.*
2. **Resuspend:** Add **250 µL Resuspension Solution** to the pellet. Pipette gently up and down until completely homogeneous with no visible clumps.
3. **Lyse:** Add **250 µL Lysis Buffer**, mix gently, and incubate at **room temperature for 5 minutes**.
4. **Neutralize:** Add **350 µL Neutralization Solution**, mix gently, and incubate on **ice for 10 minutes**.
5. **Clarify Lysate:** Centrifuge at **12,000–15,000 rpm for 10 minutes**. You will see a white, salty precipitate (cell debris/genomic DNA) and a clear supernatant containing your bacmid DNA.
6. **Second Clarification:** Carefully transfer the clear supernatant (**~850 µL**) to a fresh microcentrifuge tube, avoiding the white pellet. Centrifuge again to ensure all debris is removed.
7. **Precipitate DNA:** Transfer the clear supernatant into a new tube containing **800 µL isopropanol** (again, avoid transferring any white pellet). Mix gently and incubate on **ice for 10 minutes**.
   * *Tip: Placing the mixture at **-20°C for ~20 minutes** here improves overall yield.*
8. **Pellet DNA:** Centrifuge at **4°C for 15 minutes at max speed** (~15,000 rpm works well) to form a small white DNA pellet.
9. **Remove Supernatant:** Working quickly, immediately aspirate the supernatant with a pipette, leaving the pellet undisturbed.
   * *Troubleshooting: If no pellet is visible, incubate at -20°C again and repeat the spin.*
   * *Tip: Perform a quick **1-minute spin** after removing the bulk supernatant and pipette off any residual liquid.*
10. **Ethanol Wash:** Add **500 µL of 70% ethanol** to the pellet. Centrifuge at **max speed for 15 minutes**, then immediately remove the supernatant as before. 
    * *Tip: Spin for an additional **1–5 minutes** and remove any remaining traces of ethanol.*
11. **Dry Pellet:** Air-dry the pellet on the bench (or in a fume hood) for **10 minutes** until all residual ethanol has evaporated.
12. **Elute & Quantify:** Resuspend the dried DNA in **50 µL Elution Buffer** and quantify. Expected concentration is **1–4 µg/µL**.
